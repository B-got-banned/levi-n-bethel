FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Inside the minds of Levi & Bethel. Discover our process, tools, and how we bring digital visions to life.

Who are Levi & Bethel?

We are a creative duo specializing in digital experiences, combining technical expertise with futuristic design aesthetics. Bethel handles the Core Engineering while Levi leads the Visual Identity. Together, we build products that exist at the intersection of function and art.

What services do we provide?

We offer a cohesive ecosystem of digital services designed to elevate brands into the future. Our core offerings include End-to-End Product Development, where we take a concept from initial wireframe to a fully functional, high-performance deployment. Additionally, we specialize in Visual Systems & Branding, creating immersive identities that resonate across digital landscapes, and Interactive Motion Design to ensure every user touchpoint feels fluid and intentional.

How can we collaborate?

Our process is built on high-touch partnership. We typically begin with a Discovery Phase to align our technical roadmap with your creative vision. Whether you are a startup looking for a groundbreaking MVP or an established brand seeking a visual overhaul, we operate through agile sprints to ensure transparency and rapid iteration. You can initiate the conversation by booking a consultation through our project portal—we prioritize projects that challenge the boundaries of conventional design.

What tools do we use?

We utilize a curated stack that balances cutting-edge performance with aesthetic flexibility. For visual architecture and interface design, we live in Figma and Adobe Creative Cloud. On the engineering front, we build robust, scalable products using React, Next.js, and Three.js for immersive 3D experiences. By leveraging modern frameworks and headless CMS solutions, we ensure that the "Art" Levi creates is supported by the unbreakable "Engine" Bethel builds.
